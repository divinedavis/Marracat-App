# Marracat privacy policy — moved

This draft is retired. The one Marracat privacy policy is live at
**https://marracat.com/#/privacy**, and its source is
`web/src/PrivacyPolicy.jsx`. The iOS app's Privacy sheet
(`Marracat/Views/AccountPanel.swift`, `AccountPrivacyView`) summarises it, and
the App Store's App Privacy answers are recorded in
`app-store/app-privacy-answers.md`.

Edit the policy there, not here, so there is never a second, older copy.
