# Marracat support

Public support page: https://marracat.com/#/support

For help using Marracat, contact support@marracat.com.
Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Marracat version, and a description of the
problem. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details, or API keys.

For an order's shipping status, cancellation, refund or return, contact the brand
using the details in its order-confirmation email. Orders in the current app are
placed through each brand's checkout. Products from different brands can require
separate payments and shipments.

Prices and availability can change. The brand's checkout confirms the final
price, available inventory, shipping and taxes before you pay.
